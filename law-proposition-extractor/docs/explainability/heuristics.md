## Grouping

## `grouping.create_shared_fragment_rule_group` — version 1

**Method:** Deterministic rule

**What it does:** Law Proposition Extractor groups two or more retained propositions that share the same source_record_id, source_snapshot_id, and source_fragment_id when that fragment has a reliable canonical locator. Grouping is structural (shared source fragment), not textual similarity, shared topic, shared legal-effect classification, or model judgment. Members are ordered by deterministic source order. Single-member fragments, unreliable locators, mixed source/snapshot/fragment buckets, article-level aggregates, and descendant roll-ups do not form groups.

**Runs after / precedence:** Direct shared-fragment membership only; minimum member count is 2; canonical locator must be reliable; members sorted by source_order (with deterministic fallback).

**Trigger:** At least two retained propositions share one reliable source fragment within the same source record and snapshot.

**Outputs:** `legal_rule_group`, `decision_explanation`

**Failure behaviour:** Fewer than two members, missing source identities, unreliable locator, or source/snapshot mismatch → no group.

**Diagnostics:** _(none declared)_
